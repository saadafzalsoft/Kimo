How and where do I apply Kino?
Squeeze a pea-sized amount onto your fingertip and apply to clean, dry skin anywhere on your body — underarms, private parts, inner thighs, underboob, belly folds, or feet. Rub gently until it disappears transparently into skin.

Yes! Kino is gynecologist and dermatologist approved, doctor-formulated with gentle natural oils and skin barrier actives. It is 100% free of aluminum, alcohol, parabens, and abrasive baking soda.

Not at all. Unlike solid white sticks and yellowing aluminum antiperspirants, Kino dries clear within 10 seconds of application, keeping all your garments spotless.
